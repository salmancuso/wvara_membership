Admin access levels

Access levels are set by hand on each member's page. They are separate from club Roles (President, Treasurer and so on): holding an office does not grant access by itself.

Records Manager

For board members and the Secretary. Can update member records and admin notes, add members, confirm family links, take attendance, manage Roles, run reports and see the Member Map. Can reset a regular member's password. Cannot record or change dues, see the Activity Log, deactivate or delete members, or change anyone's access.

Club Administrator

For officers such as the President, Vice President and Treasurer. Everything a Records Manager can do, plus dues and PayPal payments, the Activity Log, deactivating or deleting members, and setting access up to Club Administrator. Cannot change a System Administrator.

System Administrator

For the people who run the tool itself. Full access, including granting or removing System Administrator.

Safety rules for everyone
  • Nobody can change, reset the password of, deactivate or delete someone at a higher level.
  • Nobody can change their own access level.
  • There is always at least one active System Administrator.
  • Every change is recorded in the Activity Log.
